Make an impact

More than softball. It's a tradition.

Since 1974, West Valley Fast Pitch has given generations of girls a place to learn the game, compete, build confidence, and be part of something bigger than themselves.

We're a volunteer-led nonprofit rooted in West Valley City and serving families across the west side of the Salt Lake Valley. Coaches, board members, parents, and neighbors give their time so every player has a positive experience on the field.

Business support lets us strengthen the league without placing every added cost on player families.

Questions

Good to know.

What does my sponsorship support?

Sponsorships help WVFP maintain and replace equipment, keep registration accessible, provide player scholarships, improve fields and training resources, support tournaments and events, and invest in the league's long-term growth.

How long does a sponsorship run?

Field banners and the Snack Shack run through the Spring and Fall 2027 seasons. Jersey sponsorships cover the season you choose. Website sponsorships run 12 months from activation, and Digital Features are one scheduled campaign. Community levels run through December 31, 2027.

Do banners include design and production?

Yes. Standard artwork, production, installation, and removal are included in featured sponsorship pricing. Just send your logo and details within 10 business days of acceptance. Nonstandard sizes, replacements, or major creative changes are handled separately.

Can I sponsor more than one thing?

Absolutely. Sponsorships are independent, so you can pair a placement — like a field banner — with a community level. Overlapping recognition is combined into one better thank-you rather than repeated.

Can my business donate goods or services instead?

Yes — we welcome equipment, food, printing, apparel, construction services, photography, and more. Recognition is based on the documented fair market value of goods or services WVFP can use, agreed in advance. In-kind gifts don't automatically include jersey, Snack Shack, or website placements.

Is my sponsorship tax deductible?

West Valley Fast Pitch, Inc. is a nonprofit corporation. The tax treatment of a sponsorship depends on its nature and the benefits you receive, so we can't guarantee deductibility. Please consult your tax advisor. We'll provide a receipt that accurately describes your payment and any benefits provided.

Can we discuss a custom partnership?

Please do. Major partnerships start at $5,000 and can include scholarships, equipment, facility improvements, training infrastructure, and multi-year commitments. Tell us what you have in mind.

When is my sponsorship confirmed?

An inquiry starts the conversation but doesn't reserve anything. Your sponsorship is confirmed once WVFP accepts it in writing and payment is received. Payment is due in full at acceptance, before production or activation, by WVFP invoice, check, or another approved method. Prior-year sponsors get a 14-day first opportunity to renew.

What if I need to cancel?

Before any production, activation, or committed expense, sponsorships are refundable less any nonrecoverable costs. After that, cancellations are reviewed case by case. If a placement becomes delayed or unavailable — because of field access, facility rules, weather, or maintenance — we'll agree in writing on a reasonable substitute, extension, or prorated adjustment.

Are there any sponsorship guidelines?

As a youth girls softball league, WVFP approves every sponsor, logo, link, and message. We don't accept sponsorship creative promoting alcohol, tobacco or nicotine, cannabis, gambling, adult content, or other age-inappropriate products. Sponsorship never includes access to player data, family contact information, or direct marketing to participants.
