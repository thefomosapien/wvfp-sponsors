About WVFP

More than softball. It's a tradition.

Since 1974, West Valley Fast Pitch has given generations of girls a place to learn the game, make friends, build confidence, and belong to something bigger than themselves.

We're a volunteer-led nonprofit rooted in West Valley City, serving families across the west side of the Salt Lake Valley. Business partners help us keep the game strong without placing every cost on player families.

Questions

Sponsorship & donation FAQ

What's the difference between a featured sponsorship and a Community Supporter?

Featured sponsorships are advertising: your business appears on field signage, jerseys, the Snack Shack, or our website. Community Supporter levels are recognition: a public thank-you on our sponsor webpage and social media, without a physical or premium placement.

What does my sponsorship support?

Sponsorships help WVFP maintain and replace equipment, keep registration accessible, build toward a future player scholarship program, improve fields and training resources, support tournaments and events, and invest in the league's long-term growth.

How long does a sponsorship run?

Field banners run for the sponsorship year, covering the Spring and Fall seasons. Jersey and Snack Shack sponsorships cover the season you choose. Website sponsorships run 12 months from activation, and a Digital Feature is one scheduled campaign. Community Supporter recognition runs for one sponsorship year. Your written confirmation spells out exact dates.

Do banners include design and production?

Yes. Standard artwork, production, installation, and removal are included in featured sponsorship pricing. Just send your logo and details within 10 business days of acceptance. Nonstandard sizes, replacements, or major creative changes are handled separately.

Can I sponsor more than one thing?

Absolutely. Sponsorships are independent, so you can combine them — for example, a Dedicated Field Banner ($750) plus Silver Community Supporter ($500) is a $1,250 partnership. Overlapping recognition is combined into one better thank-you rather than repeated.

Can my business donate goods or services instead?

Yes — we welcome equipment, food, printing, apparel, construction services, photography, and more. Recognition is based on the documented fair market value of goods or services WVFP can use, agreed in advance. In-kind gifts don't automatically include jersey, Snack Shack, or website placements.

Is my sponsorship or donation tax deductible?*

West Valley Fast Pitch, Inc. is a 501(c)⁠(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 93-1568983). Sponsorships and donations may qualify for a tax deduction, but the treatment depends on the nature of your payment and the benefits you receive, so we can't guarantee deductibility. Please consult your tax advisor. Read our plain-language tax guide.

Is a sponsorship a charitable donation or a business expense?*

It depends on what you receive in return. A Community Supporter donation, where your business is thanked by name or logo, may qualify as a charitable contribution. A featured sponsorship that promotes your business — like a field banner, jersey logo, or website placement — may instead qualify as a business advertising or marketing expense. Your tax advisor can tell you which applies to you.

Will I get a receipt for my taxes?

Yes. Once payment is received, we'll send a written acknowledgment showing the amount, the date, and a description of any recognition or benefits you received in return — the details your tax advisor will ask for.

Can my support count toward this year's taxes?*

Sponsorships and donations paid by December 31 may count toward that tax year. Because a sponsorship is confirmed only once WVFP accepts it in writing and payment is received, start your inquiry early in December. Ask your tax advisor about timing for your situation.

When is my sponsorship confirmed?

An inquiry starts the conversation but doesn't reserve anything. Your sponsorship is confirmed once WVFP accepts it in writing and payment is received. Payment is due in full at acceptance, before production or activation, by WVFP invoice, check, or another approved method. Returning sponsors get a 14-day first opportunity to renew.

What if I need to cancel?

Before any production, activation, or committed expense, sponsorships are refundable less any nonrecoverable costs. After that, cancellations are reviewed case by case. If a placement becomes delayed or unavailable — because of field access, facility rules, weather, or maintenance — we'll agree in writing on a reasonable substitute, extension, or prorated adjustment.

Are there any sponsorship guidelines?

As a youth girls softball league, WVFP approves every sponsor, logo, link, and message. We don't accept sponsorship creative promoting alcohol, tobacco or nicotine, cannabis, gambling, adult content, or other age-inappropriate products. Sponsorship never includes access to player data, family contact information, or direct marketing to participants.
