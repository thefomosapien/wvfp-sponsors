For our sponsors

Show your customers you support girls softball.

Thank you for backing West Valley Fast Pitch. Add our Proud Sponsor badge to your website so your customers know you invest in your community — it takes about two minutes.

1. Pick a badge

Use the red badge on light websites and the cream badge on dark ones. Both link back to our sponsorship page.

2. Add it to your website

  • Website builders (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, GoDaddy, Shopify): add an "HTML," "Embed," or "Custom code" block where you want the badge, and paste the code.
  • Good spots: your footer, an "About us" page, or a "Community" page.
  • Prefer an image? Download the badge, upload it like any photo, and link it to https://sponsors.wvfastpitch.org.
  • Need help? Email sponsors@wvfastpitch.org and we'll walk you or your web person through it.
